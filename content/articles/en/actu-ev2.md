Priced from €26,670, the EV2 can drop below €20,000 once incentives are deducted, depending on household income. Two batteries to choose from, up to 453 km of range, and driver aids worthy of a much more expensive car.

Kia is aiming for 10,000 sales a year in France for this four-metre model.

## Made in Slovakia, and that changes everything

Because it is assembled in Europe along with its battery, the EV2 ticks every box for French incentives. Depending on household income, its price can fall below €20,000, a rarity for an electric SUV of this size.

## Two batteries, up to 453 km

The small 42 kWh battery, paired with 147 hp, is rated at 317 km. The larger 61 kWh pack reaches 453 km with a 135 hp motor. Charging from 10 to 80% takes about 30 minutes on a fast charger.

## Clever inside

A four-seat version offers two rear seats that slide and recline independently, so you can favour legroom or luggage. A small front boot holds the cables, and the car can power an electrical appliance when camping.
