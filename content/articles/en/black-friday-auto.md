Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday 27 November, Cyber Monday on Monday the 30th. But in the car industry, the official calendar no longer means much: brands launched their promotions at the start of the month, on new vehicles as well as services and parts. Here is how to read these offers without falling into a trap.

## What manufacturers are actually offering

Most announcements concern **long-term leasing (LLD)**, often with no deposit, on selected electric and hybrid models. Audi, BMW and several mainstream brands are running this kind of offer until 30 November. Limited editions and run-out trims are the most heavily discounted.

What changes compared with a straight discount: it is not the purchase price that falls, it is the monthly payment. The two do not compare directly.

## The three figures to look at

1. **The monthly payment**, obviously — but always alongside the annual mileage included.
2. **The deposit required.** A low monthly with a €5,000 deposit is not a bargain, it is a spreading of cost.
3. **The cost per extra kilometre.** The line nobody reads, and the one that costs most after three years.

## The real lever is elsewhere

On an electric car, public incentives often weigh more than a commercial discount. Before comparing two Black Friday offers, check what the model qualifies for and what still applies on the delivery date, not the signature date. A lead time that slips into the following year can wipe out the advantage.

## Accessories and charging

This is where the discounts are most straightforward: wallboxes, cables, portable chargers, winter tyres, workshop equipment. One simple tip: on charging hardware, the Black Week offer is usually as good as Friday's, and stock goes first.

## Our verdict

Black Friday in the car world is not a moment when cars get cheaper: it is a moment when financing terms loosen. If a purchase was already planned, the window to 30 November is worth using. If it was not, an attractive monthly payment is still a three-year commitment — which has never been a good reason to buy.
