The big new feature: these projectors display 4K at 120 frames per second, the pace recent consoles demand. Controller in hand, movement stays sharp, with no smearing. In terms of light, the most powerful one can be used without plunging the room into total darkness.

Nothing complicated to set up: you put it down, aim it with its swivelling stand, and the picture lines itself up. A detail parents will appreciate: the beam switches off if someone walks in front of it.

## The range in detail

Three models share the bill, from the most affordable to the brightest. All offer Harman Kardon sound, the Google TV interface with streaming apps, and a picture from 40 to 300 inches. Depending on the model, an optical zoom and lens shift let you install it without being straight in front of the screen. In France, the mid-range model costs around €2,800.
