Previewed at IFA in Berlin in September, the XGIMI Horizon 30 series was made official on 12 October 2026. It includes three 4K projectors: Horizon 30 Pro, Horizon 30 Max and Horizon 30 Ultra Max. All use an RGB triple laser light source, with separate red, green and blue lasers.

## 4K at 120 Hz

This is the big news: all three models display 4K at 120 Hz, compared with 60 Hz on the previous generation. A 1080p 240 Hz mode is also available, with VRR, AMD FreeSync Premium and ALLM support. Input lag drops to 1.9 ms in 4K at 120 Hz: movement stays sharp, controller in hand.

## A cinema-grade picture

The triple laser source covers 110% of the BT.2020 colour space. A dual iris adjusts the light scene by scene, and the X-Master Red Ring lens limits light loss. All three models support Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and IMAX Enhanced. The image is produced by a 0.47-inch DLP DMD chip, and the Google TV interface gives access to streaming apps.

## Three models

- **Horizon 30 Pro:** 4,800 ISO lumens, 8,000:1 native contrast, €2,499
- **Horizon 30 Max:** 5,300 ISO lumens, 9,000:1 native contrast, €2,799
- **Horizon 30 Ultra Max:** up to 6,000 ISO lumens, 10,000:1 native contrast

The most powerful can be used without plunging the room into complete darkness.

## Availability

The Horizon 30 Pro and Max were announced for 1 November 2026, the Ultra Max for 11 November. Pre-order prices were offered at launch.

## Our verdict

With 4K at 120 Hz and very low latency, XGIMI finally makes the projector credible for console gaming. The Horizon 30 Pro offers the best balance, while the Ultra Max targets large, bright rooms.
