The electric Grande Panda had one weakness that was hard to argue away: 320 km of claimed range is comfortable around town, far less so on the motorway. Fiat's answer is a new 54 kWh battery that lifts range to 400 km on the WLTP cycle. And unlike most mid-life updates, the invoice does not follow the same curve.

## 80 km more, for the same use

The battery grows from 44 to 54 kWh, roughly a quarter more capacity. The claimed gain is 75 to 80 km depending on trim. On paper, that is the difference between a city car that has to plan every trip out of town and one that swallows a 150 km round trip without a second thought.

The rest of the drivetrain is untouched: a 113 hp (83 kW) motor on the front axle, enough for a car of this size and weight.

## Charging is still the weak spot

This is where Fiat chose not to overreach. DC charging peaks at 100 kW, which means around 28 minutes from 20 to 80%. Fair, no more than that: recent rivals in the segment are now closing in on 150 kW.

On AC, the Grande Panda sticks with its conventional onboard charger. For a car that will spend most nights on a domestic socket or a shared building charger, the trade-off holds up.

## Four trims, contained prices

With manufacturer discounts included, the range reads as follows:

- **Pop:** €24,400
- **Icône:** €26,300
- **Business:** €28,800
- **La Prima:** €29,300

Those figures include discounts valid until 30 November 2026, so they are worth checking at signing time. Even at full price, the Grande Panda remains one of the few family-sized EVs trading below €30,000.

## What it changes in the range

Fiat now has two arguments instead of one. Until now, the Grande Panda sold on its price and its styling, with range as the reservation. At 400 km, it joins the cars you can buy as the household's only vehicle, with no combustion back-up waiting in the driveway.

## Our verdict

This is the update we were waiting for, and it lands in the right place: on the battery, not the dashboard. The 100 kW DC ceiling will still hold back high-mileage drivers, and it is worth watching what happens to prices once the discounts expire. But at this money, with 400 km claimed, the Grande Panda becomes very hard to strike off an electric city car shortlist.
