The XPeng L03 starts at €34,990 in France, but that is not the version worth your attention. The **RWD Long Range** trim, at €37,990, combines the biggest battery in the catalogue, the best range in the line-up and a charging power few European rivals match at this money.

## Rear-wheel drive, 71.2 kWh of LFP

The L03 Long Range gets a 71.2 kWh LFP battery and a 180 kW rear motor, or 245 hp. Homologated range reaches 520 km WLTP on 18-inch wheels — the Long Range Ultra, on 20-inch rims, drops back to 480 km. Top speed is limited to 180 km/h on all rear-drive versions.

Choosing rear-wheel drive is not just about power: without a front motor, the L03 saves around a hundred kilos and gains a more generous frunk. It is the best-balanced version of the range.

## 236 kW: the real argument

Rapid charging peaks at 236 kW, enough to go from 10 to 80% in around twenty minutes. Below €40,000, that is a level you rarely find elsewhere: many rivals in the segment top out between 100 and 150 kW.

On a long trip, that difference is measured in shorter stops rather than extra kilometres — and that is often what makes a good motorway car.

## Size and packaging

The L03 is 4.70 m long, in the format of a family coupé-SUV. The boot is quoted at 367 litres, a modest figure for the class and a direct consequence of that falling roofline.

## The range in France

- **RWD Standard Range:** €34,990 — 58.3 kWh, 445 km
- **RWD Long Range:** €37,990 — 71.2 kWh, 520 km
- **RWD Long Range Ultra:** €42,990 — 71.2 kWh, 480 km
- **AWD Performance Ultra:** €46,990 — 71.2 kWh, 440 km

The €3,000 gap between Standard Range and Long Range buys 75 km of extra range and a bigger battery. It is comfortably the best value in the grid.

## Our verdict

XPeng has put together one of the most coherent offers under €40,000 right now: rear-wheel drive, real range and rapid charging that holds up. The boot remains the weak point, and the resale value of a brand still young in Europe is an unknown. But on paper, at this price, the L03 Long Range puts a lot of rivals in difficulty.
