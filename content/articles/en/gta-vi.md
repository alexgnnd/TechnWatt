We thought it would never arrive. Announced for 2025, pushed back to 26 May 2026 and then to 19 November, the sixth Grand Theft Auto finally lands this Thursday on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No date has been set yet for the PC version. A friendly tip: make room on your console, the game needs more than 200 GB.

## Lucia and Jason, a first for the series

For the first time, the series features two playable heroes: Lucia Caminos, the first female lead in a main GTA game, and Jason Duval. The starting point is classic for the saga: an easy job that goes wrong and drags them into something much bigger.

## Welcome to Leonida

The story takes place in Leonida, a fictional state that looks remarkably like Florida. At its heart is Vice City, Miami's sunny double. Around it lie the Keys, a string of islands, and the Grassrivers swamps, home to alligators. Seafront, six-lane highways, country roads: a playground made for convertible cruising.

## The biggest car park in gaming

In GTA, the real star is often the car. Fans have already counted more than 200 different models by analysing the trailers frame by frame. True to tradition, Rockstar uses no real brands. Each car blends several existing models: close enough to recognise a German sports car or a luxury saloon, different enough not to upset any manufacturer. Some names return, such as the Grotti Cheetah Classic, spotted in white in the trailer.

## Classic car hunters, get ready

A new feature that car lovers will appreciate: a classic car collection. The idea is to track down abandoned vehicles, restore them and keep them in your garage. You can also hitch a trailer or a boat, speed along in a powerboat and even paddle a kayak.

## Two editions

The game comes in a Standard Edition and an Ultimate Edition. The latter adds premium vehicles, including the Dinka Enduro motorcycle and the Crest kayak, as well as a Ganado Retro Build modkit. Digital purchases made before 20 November include the Vintage Vice City Pack.

## And what about electric cars?

That is the question we are itching to ask. The previous game already slipped in a few silent electric sports cars, a clear nod to a famous Californian brand. For Vice City, it is a mystery: the trailers are mostly full of roaring V8s. Will charging stations have a place in Leonida? Find out on Thursday.
