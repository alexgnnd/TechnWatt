We thought it would never arrive. Delayed twice, first from 2025 to spring 2026, then to the autumn, the sixth instalment finally lands on 19 November on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. A friendly tip: make room on your console, the game needs more than 200 GB. PC players will have to wait a little longer.

## Welcome to Florida… almost

The story takes place in Leonida, an imaginary state that looks remarkably like Florida. At its heart is Vice City, Miami's sunny double. Seafront, six-lane highways, alligator swamps: a playground made for convertible cruises.

![](img/gta-vice-city.jpg)

## The biggest car park in gaming

In GTA, the real star is often the car. Fans have already counted more than 200 different models by combing through the trailers frame by frame. True to tradition, Rockstar uses no real brands: each car blends several existing models, close enough that you recognise a German sports car or a luxury saloon, different enough not to upset any manufacturer. Real design work.

![](img/gta-pickup.jpg)

Little extras that make a difference: you will be able to hitch a trailer or a boat, race an airboat through the swamps and even paddle a kayak.

## And what about electric cars?

That is the question on our minds. The previous game already slipped in a few silent electric sports cars, a pointed nod to a famous Californian brand. For Vice City, it is a mystery: the trailers mostly feature roaring V8s. Will charging points have a place in Leonida? See you on Thursday.
