Priced at €24,995, exactly the price of a Renault 5, the ID. Polo is built in Spain. It promises up to 450 km of range and a bigger boot than a Golf.

For those who want thrills, a 226 hp ID. Polo GTI follows this autumn, fifty years after the very first GTI.

## Two batteries for two uses

The entry version has an LFP battery of about 37 kWh, good for a little over 300 km: perfect for town and the suburbs. The 52 kWh battery aims for 450 km and accepts up to 105 kW on a fast charger. Power ranges from 116 to 211 hp, and the strongest version does 0 to 100 km/h in just over 7 seconds.

## An interior that turns the page

Volkswagen has listened to the criticism: real buttons are back on the steering wheel, there is one switch per window, and softer materials cover the dashboard. The driver display can mimic the dials of an old-school Golf. The fabrics are made from recycled plastic, some of it recovered from the sea.

## The GTI, fifty years on

To celebrate half a century of the first Golf GTI, Volkswagen is launching a 226 hp ID. Polo GTI. It keeps the codes: red stripes, sports seats, a firmer chassis and 0 to 100 km/h in 6.8 seconds.
