Priced from €24,995 in Trend trim, the ID. Polo lines up exactly with the Renault 5 E-Tech electric. Volkswagen is not just plugging in its Polo: it debuts the MEB+ platform, an evolution of the ID.3 and ID.4 base designed for small front-wheel-drive models.

## Polo size, bigger-class space

At 4.05 m long, 1.82 m wide and with a 2.60 m wheelbase, the ID. Polo is almost as long as the combustion Polo. But with its front-mounted motor and battery in the floor, it frees up space: the boot offers 441 litres. The careful design also helps efficiency, with a drag coefficient of around 0.264.

## Two batteries, two philosophies

The 85 kW (116 hp) and 99 kW (135 hp) versions get a 37 kWh net LFP (lithium iron phosphate) battery. Cheaper and known for its durability, it offers up to around 329 km of WLTP range and accepts up to 90 kW on a fast charger. The 155 kW (211 hp) versions and the 166 kW (226 hp) GTI move to a 52 kWh NMC (nickel manganese cobalt) battery with higher energy density. It extends the range to 454 km and charges at up to 105 kW on direct current.

In both cases, 10 to 80% takes around 23 to 24 minutes on a fast charger. On alternating current, all versions accept 11 kW. The cells are supplied by PowerCo, the Volkswagen Group's battery company.

## An interior that turns the page

Volkswagen has listened to the criticism of its first ID. models. Real buttons are back on the steering wheel, there is a switch for each window and nicer materials on the dashboard. The infotainment screen measures 12.9 inches (32.7 cm) and the instrument cluster is digital, with a display that can mimic an old Golf.

## Standard driver assistance

Even the entry-level ID. Polo gets Side Assist and Lane Assist with Emergency Assist. As an option, Connected Travel Assist keeps the car in its lane, handles assisted lane changes on the motorway and recognises traffic lights and stop signs. A digital key on your smartphone is also on the options list.

## Trend, Life, Style… and GTI

The range starts with the Trend trim, followed by Life and Style. Style adds IQ.LIGHT LED Matrix headlights, an illuminated light strip at the front, 3D LED tail lights and an illuminated VW logo front and rear. For thrill-seekers, the 226 hp ID. Polo GTI keeps the historic codes: red touches, sports seats and a firmer chassis, fifty years after the first Golf GTI.

## Our verdict

A competitive starting price, a generous boot and a real overhaul of the ergonomics: the ID. Polo has strong arguments against the Renault 5. The choice of battery will be key. The 37 kWh LFP suits mostly urban use, while the 52 kWh is the one to pick if you regularly take the motorway.
