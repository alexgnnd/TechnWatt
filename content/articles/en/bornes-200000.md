---
titre: '200,000 chargers: a breather, but only just'
categorie: Society · Charging
chapo: The symbolic milestone was passed this summer. Good news. Except that behind the scenes, the machine is slowing down.
---

This year, about a quarter fewer chargers were installed than over the same period in 2025. At this pace, the government's target for 2030, doubling the network, looks more and more like wishful thinking. Another stone in the shoe: about one charger in ten is out of order at any given time.

For drivers, the picture remains fairly reassuring: you can almost always find a plug. The real challenge for the coming years will be keeping them in working order.
