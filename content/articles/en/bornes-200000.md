titre: '200,000 charging points in France: milestone reached, but the pace is slowing'

chapo: France now has more than 200,000 public charging points. A symbolic milestone, as the pace of installation slows and chargers have never been used so much.
---
It is official: France has passed the milestone of 200,000 public charging points. According to the Avere-France barometer, published with the Ministry for Ecological Transition, there were exactly 200,045 on 31 July 2026, then 200,926 a month later. It took three years to go from 100,000 to 200,000.

## A slowing pace

The milestone has been reached, but momentum is fading. In the first half of 2026, 14,544 new points were commissioned, compared with 19,880 over the same period in 2025: around a quarter fewer. Year on year, growth was still 13% at the end of August.

The problem is the target. The government is aiming for 400,000 public points by 2030, including 50,000 high-power ones. To get there, far more chargers would need to be installed each half-year, not fewer.

## Where are the chargers?

Almost half of public points (47%) are at commercial sites: supermarkets, shopping centres, restaurants. Next come public or private car parks open to all (30%), on-street charging, mainly in cities (13%), and companies that have opened their chargers to the public (4%). In terms of power, just over 5,000 points deliver at least 350 kW, enough to charge the fastest cars in around twenty minutes.

## Chargers are used more and more

The real change shows in usage. In July 2026, each charging point hosted an average of 38.6 sessions in the month, a record. In spring, the energy delivered per point had already jumped 68% year on year. More electric cars on the road means better-used chargers… and sometimes queues on the motorway during the holidays.

## Reliability, the next challenge

Another point to watch: availability. In July, 89% of points were technically available. In other words, around one charger in ten was not working at any given moment.

## And at home?

Charging is also growing in apartment buildings: more than 44,000 points were installed in shared residential buildings in the second quarter of 2026, up 38% year on year.

## Our verdict

For drivers, the picture remains reassuring: you can almost always find a plug. But to keep up with booming sales, the network needs to speed up its roll-out and become more reliable. That is the challenge for the years ahead.
