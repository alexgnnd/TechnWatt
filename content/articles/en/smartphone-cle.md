The little winter-morning ritual is changing. From the kitchen, you start the heating while the car is still plugged in: you set off in a warm cabin without eating into the range. In the evening, you schedule charging to take advantage of off-peak rates. More and more models, like the ID. Polo as an option, even leave the key in the drawer: the phone is enough.

## How it works

The car recognises the phone from a few metres away using Bluetooth or a very precise location chip. On some models, such as the Kia EV2, the key goes straight into the digital wallet of an iPhone or Android smartphone, next to your bank card. You can even lend your car remotely by sending a temporary key to a friend or relative.
