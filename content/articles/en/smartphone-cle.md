The morning routine is changing. You walk up to the car and it unlocks; you sit down and it starts, without ever taking out a key. More and more manufacturers offer a digital key stored in your smartphone or smartwatch.

## A common standard: CCC Digital Key

Most of these keys are based on the CCC Digital Key standard, developed by the Car Connectivity Consortium, which brings together more than 300 companies, including Apple, Google and many car makers. In 2025, 115 vehicles and modules received CCC Digital Key certification. The result: a compatible key works on both iPhone and Android, and can be shared across platforms.

## How it works

Three technologies work together. Bluetooth Low Energy handles communication and authentication. Ultra Wideband (UWB) measures the phone's distance and position very precisely, allowing secure hands-free unlocking. NFC lets you unlock by holding the phone against the door handle, and keeps working when the phone's battery is low.

## In your phone's wallet

The key sits directly in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, next to your bank card. Express Mode even lets you use it without Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode, an option you can turn off. You can also lend your car remotely by sending a key to someone, then revoke it at any time.

## Who offers it?

BMW offers its Digital Key Plus on iPhone and Android. Volvo extended it in 2026 to its EX30, EX60, ES90 and EX90. Kia includes it in the new EV2 with Kia Digital Key, and Volkswagen offers it as an option on the ID. Polo. Note, though, that each manufacturer chooses which features to enable, and not all digital keys follow the full standard.

## And for electric cars?

The manufacturer's app often complements the key: from the kitchen, you start preheating while the car is still plugged in, so you leave with a warm cabin without using up range. In the evening, you schedule charging to take advantage of off-peak hours.

## Our verdict

The digital key is one of those discreet innovations that really changes everyday life. Before buying, just check that your phone and the car's version are compatible.
