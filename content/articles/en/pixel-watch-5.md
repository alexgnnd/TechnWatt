Two sizes, 41 and 45 mm, for small and large wrists, and a round design that contrasts with Apple's square. Google presents it as its most polished watch for tracking activity and sleep. Price-wise, it starts just below its rival.

## Who is it for?

For Android phone users who want to track their workouts, sleep and heart rate without changing ecosystem. It pairs naturally with a Pixel, but works with most Android smartphones. Allow €30 more for the large model.
