Unveiled on 12 August 2026 alongside the Pixel 11 phones, the Google Pixel Watch 5 has been shipping since 21 August. It keeps the round, dome-shaped design of the previous generation, in two sizes: 41 and 45 mm.

## A brighter screen

The curved Actua 360 display reaches up to 3,000 nits, to stay readable in bright sunlight. It is protected by custom 3D Corning Gorilla Glass 5. The watch is water resistant to 5 ATM and IP68 rated. The case is made of 100% recycled aluminium.

## Battery and charging

Google claims up to 30 hours for the 41 mm model and up to 40 hours for the 45 mm. In battery saver mode, that rises to 48 and 72 hours. With the Quick Charge Dock, 15 minutes is enough to get 15 hours of battery life.

## Health tracking

The Pixel Watch 5 tracks workouts, sleep and heart rate, with AI helping to interpret the data. Tracking now goes through the Google Health app, which replaces the Fitbit app. Depending on the country, satellite SOS is also available.

## Who is it for?

For Android users who want a full-featured watch without changing ecosystem. It pairs naturally with a Pixel, but works with most recent Android phones.

## Prices in France

- **41 mm Wi-Fi:** €419
- **45 mm Wi-Fi:** €449
- **41 mm LTE:** €519
- **45 mm LTE:** €549

The 41 mm model is €20 more than the Pixel Watch 4, while the 45 mm keeps the same price. A Steph Curry special edition, with a band designed with the NBA player, completes the range.

## Our verdict

The Pixel Watch 5 is an evolution rather than a revolution: same design, almost identical battery life, but a brighter screen. It remains one of the best watches for Android users.
