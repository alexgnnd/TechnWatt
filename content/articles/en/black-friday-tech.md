Black Friday Week 2026 started on Thursday 19 November at Amazon, with several retailers already running promotions. Black Friday itself falls on the 27th, Cyber Monday on the 30th. Ten days of deals, during which not everything falls the same way. Here is how to sort through it.

## Projectors: the most heavily discounted category

Historically this is where the gaps are most spectacular, because list prices are elastic the rest of the year. [Portable Google TV projectors](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=videoprojecteur+portable+google+tv) routinely lose 30 to 40% during this period, and it is rarely an illusion: these are products whose list price does not hold for twelve months.

The useful reflex: look at real brightness in ANSI lumens rather than the marketing figure, and check that Netflix is properly certified on the model.

## Smartwatches: previous generations collapse

This is when last year's models genuinely drop, often by half. [Samsung Galaxy Watch](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=montre+connectee+samsung+galaxy+watch) and previous-generation [Google Pixel Watch](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=google+pixel+watch) models follow the same curve as soon as the new one settles into the catalogue.

For daily use — notifications, sleep, activity — one generation's difference is barely noticeable. This is the category where Black Friday makes the most sense.

## Smartphones: beware the fake discounts

The most misleading category. Recent iPhones and Galaxys rarely come down more than 10 to 15%, and many "offers" run through loyalty cards, trade-in bonuses or cashback schemes that artificially inflate the headline gap.

The right method: compare against the price actually charged in recent weeks, not the launch price. On [smartphones](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=smartphone), the real falls mostly concern mid-range models and the previous generation.

## Audio and smart home: the best discount-to-usefulness ratio

[Noise-cancelling earbuds](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=ecouteurs+reduction+de+bruit) and smart home accessories — connected bulbs, plugs, media players — show straightforward, immediate reductions with no deferred refund mechanism. [Media players](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=fire+tv+stick+4k+max) are the textbook case: the price drops, it drops immediately, and there is no form to fill in.

## Three reflexes to keep

1. **Check the price history** before clicking: a tracking tool tells you in ten seconds whether the discount is real.
2. **Be wary of conditional discounts**: loyalty cards, cashback, trade-in bonuses. The saving is real, but it is not immediate.
3. **Do not wait for Friday the 27th.** On many products the floor price is reached during Black Week, and stock goes first.

## Our verdict

Black Friday remains the best moment of the year to buy a projector, audio gear or connected devices. For a recent smartphone it is almost always a false good idea: the real drop comes in spring. And the best advice is still the dullest — only buy what you had already planned to buy.
