---
titre: The Norwegians watch us go by
categorie: Society · Market
chapo: In France, 41% of new cars sold in October were electric. A record we are proud of… until we look north.
---

In Norway, electric cars now account for almost all new car sales. Denmark is at around four in five. Their secret is no secret: years of very generous tax breaks and a charging network built very early. France, for its part, is catching up fast: the electric share has almost doubled in two years.
