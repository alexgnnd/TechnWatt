€19,490 before incentives, and around €15,000 for households that receive the maximum bonus: the Twingo becomes one of the cheapest new electric cars on the market. Developed in under two years, it is built in Slovenia.

Its 27.5 kWh battery is clearly aimed at town driving, with 263 km of range. Note that fast charging is an option.

## A cult face returns

Round headlights, a smiling face, flared wings: the new Twingo openly winks at the first generation of 1993. It is 3.79 m long, keeps five doors and a sliding rear bench that takes the boot up to 360 litres.

## Made for the city

With 82 hp and a 27.5 kWh battery, the Twingo is not after performance but frugality: around 12 kWh per 100 km, one of the lowest figures on the market. A turning circle under 10 metres makes it a parking champion. On the motorway, however, the range drops quickly.

## The thing to watch

Fast charging is not standard. Without the optional pack of about €500, you cannot plug into a motorway charger. With it, getting back to 80% takes just under half an hour.
