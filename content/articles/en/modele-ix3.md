Up to 805 km claimed: on paper, Nantes to Lyon in one go. The first Neue Klasse model, it debuts the sixth generation of BMW eDrive and an 800 V architecture. It is built in Debrecen, Hungary, in a plant designed for the Neue Klasse and powered by 100% renewable electricity.

## Behind the wheel

469 hp, all-wheel drive and 4.9 seconds from 0 to 100 km/h: the iX3 can pin you to your seat. But the most interesting part lies elsewhere: the Heart of Joy computer, together with the BMW Dynamic Performance Control software, controls the drivetrain, braking, energy recovery and steering far more finely than before, with a function that smooths every stop until the car is completely still.

## Consumption

On paper, 15.1 to 17.9 kWh/100 km, a remarkable result for a 4.78 m SUV. In real life, mixed-use readings range between 17 and 20 kWh/100 km, leaving 550 to 650 km of range. On the motorway, it stays around 22 to 23 kWh/100 km, or close to 480 km between stops.

## Screens and software

This is the real revolution. The instrument cluster disappears, replaced by BMW Panoramic iDrive: a light strip running along the base of the windscreen, from pillar to pillar. You keep your eyes on the road and the information comes to you. In the centre, a screen of about 18 inches and a 3D head-up display. It all runs on BMW Operating System X, highly customisable and updated over the air, with games via AirConsole and video streaming to pass the time while charging. Under the skin, four computing "super-brains" share the work: handling, assisted driving, infotainment and comfort. BMW is also preparing a voice assistant capable of real conversation, but it will come to Germany and the United States first.

## Driver assistance

The computer dedicated to assisted driving is twenty times more powerful than before. On the motorway, a glance in the mirror is enough to confirm a lane change suggested by the car, and the satnav itself suggests moving over as you approach an exit.

## Materials and finish

The cabin turns the page on wood trim and endless buttons: a clean dashboard, premium materials, but still a few hard plastics in the lower areas. About a third of the car is made of recycled materials, including all the carpets and the headliner.

## Seating and boot

The generous wheelbase benefits rear passengers, who are better off than in the petrol X3. The 520-litre boot grows to 1,750 litres with the seats folded, and the 58-litre front compartment easily swallows the cables and a bag.

## Charging

On a 400 kW charger, ten minutes are enough to add around 372 km, and 10 to 80% takes 21 minutes. The catch: these chargers are still rare in France. At home, the 11 kW charger is standard, and a 22 kW charger that can give energy back is optional.
