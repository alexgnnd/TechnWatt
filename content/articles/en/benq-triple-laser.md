The first targets gamers: its response time is so short you forget you are playing on a projector, and it can be controlled along with the rest of the smart home. The second focuses on colour for film fans, with a motorised lens you adjust from the sofa. French prices have not yet been announced.

## The two models

The TK715i, for the living room, relies on the Google TV interface, a bright 4K picture and a display lag of just a few milliseconds. The W4200, for home cinemas, focuses on colour accuracy and supports the latest HDR formats. Both have an artificial intelligence chip that adjusts the picture and helps with installation.
