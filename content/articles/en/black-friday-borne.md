Every year, Black Friday knocks 10 to 40% off wallbox prices. Every year too, buyers discover afterwards that the charger was only half the budget. In 2026, one more factor comes into play: the French tax credit for home charger installation, the CIBRE, is under review in the finance bill. Here is how to think it through before clicking.

## The dates to remember

- **Week of 23 November:** "Black Week", when most offers are already live.
- **Friday 27 November:** Black Friday itself.
- **Monday 30 November:** Cyber Monday, the last wave of deals.

There is no point waiting for Friday morning: on charging hardware, many products already hit their floor price during Black Week, and stock runs out before the weekend.

## Separate the charger from the installation

This is the distinction that changes everything. The charger itself is a product like any other: it goes on sale, sometimes heavily. The installation requires a qualified IRVE electrician and is not discounted. On a total budget, fitting often accounts for half the invoice.

Buying a cut-price wallbox without having the installation quoted means risking the discovery that your consumer unit, the distance to the meter or the car park layout blows up the estimate.

## The point to check before signing

The tax credit for buying and fitting a home charger benefited nearly 47,000 households last year. The 2026 finance bill plans to call it into question, and its fate depends on the final text. For an installation paid for in 2026, the question is worth asking the installer directly, quote in hand, rather than discovering the answer at tax time.

## What is actually worth it

- **A 7.4 kW single-phase charger** covers the vast majority of domestic uses: one night is enough to fill any battery.
- **A 22 kW charger** only makes sense if the property has a three-phase supply *and* the car accepts that power on AC — which is far from systematic.
- **A portable cable** remains the cheapest back-up solution, but does not replace a fixed installation for daily use.

## Where to look during Black Week

- [Type 2 home chargers](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=borne+de+recharge+type+2+7%2C4+kW) — most discounts land on 7.4 kW single-phase models.
- [Portable chargers and Type 2 cables](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=cable+de+recharge+type+2+voiture+electrique) — useful as a back-up, chosen for the length and amperage actually available to you.
- [Reinforced sockets and fitting accessories](https://www.amazon.fr/s?k=prise+renforcee+voiture+electrique) — not to be confused with a proper wallbox.

## Our verdict

Black Friday is a good moment to buy the hardware, not to improvise a project. The order that works: get the installation quoted first, check the power your car can actually use, then watch for the discount on the model you picked. Taken that way round, the discount is a bonus. Taken backwards, it often ends up costing more.
