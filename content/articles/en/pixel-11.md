The main sensor has grown and lets in much more light, ready to shine in dimly lit evenings. The Pro versions add a 5x zoom and can film in 8K. On the assistant side, Gemini translates a video or voice message live and turns hesitant dictation into clean text.

Good news: every model starts with 256 GB, no more phones that fill up quickly. Less good news: the bill rises by about a hundred euros compared with last year, with €999 for the base model.

## Day to day

A new built-in search finds anything on the phone, a photo, a message, a setting, when you describe it in your own words. The Pro models get a very bright screen that stays readable even in full sunlight. Wi-Fi 7 and the latest magnetic wireless charging are on board, and every model resists water and dust. The Pros even get a small light on the back to signal notifications without turning on the screen.
