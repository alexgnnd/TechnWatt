Google unveiled the Pixel 11 range on 12 August 2026. It has four models: Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. All share the same new in-house chip, the Google Tensor G6, and now start with 256 GB of storage.

## Tensor G6, built on 2 nm

The Tensor G6 is built on a 2 nm process and paired with the Titan M3 security coprocessor. It runs the latest Gemini Nano model directly on the phone to handle voice, images, translation and contextual actions. The Pixel 11 phones ship with Android 17 and will be updated for seven years.

## Gemini Intelligence

Under the name Gemini Intelligence, Google groups its AI features: translation, editing, search through your own data… A small LED even lights up on the back to show that Gemini is listening, thinking or answering. Note: some of these features are not yet available in France at launch.

## Photography

On the Pro models, the 5x telephoto allows Super Zoom up to 30x, and Pro Res Zoom goes up to 120x thanks to Google's imaging models. Creators also get Creator Mode and Pro Stable Video stabilisation.

## The Pixel 11 in detail

The base model gets a 6.3-inch Actua OLED screen, from 60 to 120 Hz, with a peak brightness of 3,000 nits. It has 12 GB of RAM, is IP68 water and dust resistant and weighs 197 g. For charging, Pixelsnap adds magnets on the back and Qi2.2 wireless charging up to 25 W. A new accessory, Pixel Tag, helps you find your belongings.

## Prices in France

- **Pixel 11:** €999 (256 GB)
- **Pixel 11 Pro:** €1,199 (256 GB)
- **Pixel 11 Pro XL:** €1,399 (256 GB)
- **Pixel 11 Pro Fold:** €1,999 (256 GB)

The Pixel 11 comes in Frost, Pistachio, Fuchsia and Obsidian.

## Our verdict

Google refines an already solid formula: more storage, a more efficient chip and seven years of updates. But the price rise is noticeable, and some Gemini features are still on their way to France.
