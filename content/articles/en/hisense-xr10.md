The XR10 is clearly aimed at home cinema enthusiasts. Its lens has around fifteen glass elements, and it handles the picture formats of the best streaming films. You still need the wall and the room to go with it: this is a device costing almost €6,000.

## Under the hood

A triple-laser light source, a latest-generation display chip able to switch its micro-mirrors thousands of times per second, and a very wide colour palette. It supports Dolby Vision and IMAX Enhanced, found on many streaming films. Unveiled at the Las Vegas show in January, it arrived in France this summer.
