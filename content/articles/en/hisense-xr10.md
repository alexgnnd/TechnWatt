Unveiled at CES in Las Vegas in January 2026, the Hisense XR10 has been available in France since the summer, priced at €5,999. It is the brand's most ambitious projector.

## Up to 300 inches

The XR10 projects a 4K Ultra HD image from 80 to 300 inches, or 2 to 7.6 metres diagonally. Its RGB triple laser source delivers 6,000 ANSI lumens and covers 118% of the BT.2020 colour space. The lasers are rated for 25,000 hours.

## High-end optics

Its lens has 17 glass elements. The 2.38x optical zoom and motorised lens shift let you install it without being directly in front of the screen. A seven-level adjustable iris improves contrast, and a liquid cooling system makes it very quiet.

## Every format

The XR10 supports Dolby Vision, HDR10+, HDR10 and HLG, and is IMAX Enhanced certified, whose expanded frame shows up to 26% more picture. It also offers Filmmaker Mode, several Cinema modes and a Sport mode. In game mode, it goes up to 240 Hz.

## Sound by Devialet

Hisense developed the 2.1 audio system with France's Devialet, and tuned it with help from the Opéra de Paris. It supports Dolby Digital and DTS:X. The interface runs on Hisense's VIDAA system.

## Our verdict

A huge, very bright and colourful picture: the XR10 ticks every home cinema box. But at this price, you also need the room, the wall and the screen to go with it.
