Closed, it is a compact iPhone with a 5.4-inch screen. Open, you get a 7.6-inch mini tablet that you can even scribble on with the Apple Pencil, a first. The crease in the middle is very discreet, and the whole device stays water-resistant.

To get there, Apple made some bold cuts. No face recognition: you unlock with your finger on the side button. No powerful optical zoom either, just two 48 MP camera sensors. A physical SIM card? Forget it. A clever little touch: the outer screen shows the framing to the person you are photographing.

## Under the hood

It shares the chip of the iPhone 18 Pro and debuts a new in-house modem, faster and less power-hungry. Each half of the phone hides its own battery. With iOS 27, you can display two apps side by side, as on an iPad. Storage ranges from 256 GB to 2 TB, and MagSafe wireless charging is kept. A well-thought-out feature for group photos: the phone takes the shot by itself when everyone is ready.
